Background

The Story Behind the Work

I taught myself to invest by reading annual reports, studying balance sheets, and putting real money on the line in the Nigerian Stock Exchange. That process — the confusion, the breakthroughs, the compounding — became the foundation for everything I build today.

Now I run Finspire, an investment education brand that takes people from "I don't know where to start" to making informed decisions with their own money. I also manage a private investment fund and I'm building an AI-powered equity research platform for the Nigerian market.

Services

What I Do

Investment Education

Paid cohort-based courses and free workshops that teach retail investors how to analyse stocks, build portfolios, and think long-term. Over 10 cohorts delivered through the Finspire programme.
